/ Event info

Important information about our events.

Read this before you come out. It covers what to wear, who gets in and when to show up.

/ The basics

Every White Out works the same way.

Dress code
All white, head to toe.It is strict at the door. If you are not in white, you may not get in.
Age
18+ to get in. 21+ to drink.Every event is 18+.
ID
Bring valid ID.You will be asked for it at the door.
Hours
10 PM to 2 AM.
Where
A bar or venue near campus.Each school has its own night and its own venue.
Who can come
All students.Your hometown friends are welcome too.
Music
Open format. A bit of everything.Requests are taken.
/ Tickets

Ticket policy.

Final sale
All tickets are final sale.Tickets are not refundable or exchangeable if your plans change.
If we cancel
You get a full refund.If an event is cancelled and not rescheduled, everything you paid, including fees, goes back to your original payment method.
Early bird
Early bird tickets are limited.Tickets are sold in price tiers. Earlier tiers cost less, and the price goes up as each one sells out.
Your ticket
One ticket is one entry for one person.Your QR code lets in whoever shows it first, one time. Keep it private.
ID
Government photo ID showing your date of birth.A student card by itself is not proof of age.
At the door
Entry depends on capacity and the door policy.Staff can refuse entry or remove anyone for intoxication, unsafe or disrespectful behaviour, or failing the ID check. In that case the ticket is not refunded.
Where to buy
Only buy tickets through this site.We are not responsible for tickets bought from anyone else.
