/ Event info

Important information about our events.

Read this before you come out. It covers what to wear, who gets in and when to show up.

/ The basics

Every White Out works the same way.

Dress code
All white, head to toe.It is strict at the door. If you are not in white, you may not get in.
Age
18+ to get in. 21+ to drink.Every event is 18+.
ID
Bring valid ID.You will be asked for it at the door.
Hours
10 PM to 2 AM.
Where
A bar or venue near campus.Each school has its own night and its own venue.
Who can come
All students.Your hometown friends are welcome too.
Music
Open format. A bit of everything.Requests are taken.
