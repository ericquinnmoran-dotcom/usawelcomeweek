The biggest first-year party of the year. The North Carolina A&T State University White Out is calling all North Carolina A&T State University first years to kick off the school year. A packed dance floor, lights, deep bass, and everyone dressed head to toe in all white. No colours, no exceptions. This is the night to meet your new classmates, link up with your floormates and find your people. Early bird tickets are limited. All tickets are final sale.
