/ FAQ

Frequently asked questions.

What is the dress code?
All white, head to toe. It is strictly enforced at the door.
Who can come?
All students are welcome, and your hometown friends are too.
How old do I have to be?
18+ to get in. Every event is 18+.
Can I drink?
18+ to enter, 21+ to drink. Valid ID is required.
What time does it start and end?
10 PM to 2 AM.
Where is it?
At a bar or venue near campus. Each school has its own night and its own venue.
What is the music?
Open format, a bit of everything. Requests are taken.
Can I get a refund?
All tickets are final sale. If we cancel an event and it is not rescheduled, you get a full refund of everything you paid, including fees.
What ID do I need?
Current government photo ID that shows your date of birth. A student card by itself is not proof of age.
Do ticket prices go up?
Yes. Early bird tickets are limited, and the price goes up as each tier sells out.
Can someone else use my ticket?
One ticket is one entry for one person. The QR code lets in whoever shows it first, one time, so keep it private.
Can I promote?
Yes. DM @ericmoran__ on Instagram to join the team.
